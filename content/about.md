I'm Vince Wadhwani. I run [Econify](https://www.econify.com), a software consulting company in New York, and before that I worked at Apple on WiFi and the iMac.

I think success is not how much you accumulate. It's how well you live, what you create, and how much better you leave the world for others. That's what I write about here, and the posts fall into four sections.

**The Good Life** is about happiness, kindness, creativity, and living in the present instead of chasing someone else's idea of success.

**The Good Company** is about leadership, rewarding people who do great work, developing people who'll lead after you, and remembering that a business has more stakeholders than its shareholders.

**The Good Society** is about shared prosperity, the environment, and the institutions everyone depends on.

**Curiosity & Creation** is technology, AI, music, and projects I build because they're fun, not because they need to make money.

There are also a lot of old Linux tutorials here. Most started as notes to myself. Feel free to share or copy anything, just link back to the original.
