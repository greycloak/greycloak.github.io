Effective date: October 19, 2026

Doris is a personal AI assistant built and run by Vince Wadhwani for his own use. This policy explains what data Doris accesses and how it is handled.

**What Doris accesses.** With the owner's consent through Google's OAuth sign-in, Doris can read and send Gmail, read and manage Google Calendar events, and access Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts. Doris acts only on requests from its owner.

**How data is used.** Google data is used only to answer the owner's requests and carry out tasks he asks for, such as summarizing email, checking his calendar, or drafting a reply. Doris does not use Google data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models.

**Where data is stored.** Google OAuth tokens and Doris's notes and session history are stored on a private server owned by Vince. Google content is not stored beyond what the owner's requests need.

**Who else sees it.** When a request involves email or calendar content, that content is sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) to generate a response. Nothing else is shared with third parties.

**Limited Use.** Doris's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Revoking access.** You can revoke Doris's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can go to Vince Wadhwani through the contact options on the [About page](/about/).

**Changes.** This policy may be updated. The effective date above will change when it does.
