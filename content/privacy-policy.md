Effective date: November 17, 2026

Doris is a personal AI assistant built and run by Vince Wadhwani for his own use. This policy explains what data Doris accesses and how it is handled.

**What Doris accesses.** With the owner's consent through each service's own sign-in (OAuth), Doris can read and send Gmail, read and manage Google Calendar events, and access Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts. It can also read the owner's own data from other services he connects, such as his Oura Ring account (sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, and similar health data). Doris acts only on requests from its owner.

**How data is used.** Google and Oura data is used only to answer the owner's requests and carry out tasks he asks for, such as summarizing email, checking his calendar, drafting a reply, or reviewing his sleep trends. Doris does not use this data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models.

**Where data is stored.** OAuth tokens and Doris's notes and session history are stored on a private server owned by Vince. Content from connected services is not stored beyond what the owner's requests need, except health summaries the owner asks Doris to keep.

**Who else sees it.** When a request involves email, calendar, or health data, that data is sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) to generate a response. Nothing else is shared with third parties.

**Limited Use.** Doris's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Revoking access.** You can revoke Doris's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) for Google, and from your Oura account's connected apps settings for Oura.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can go to Vince Wadhwani through the contact options on the [About page](/about/).

**Changes.** This policy may be updated. The effective date above will change when it does.
